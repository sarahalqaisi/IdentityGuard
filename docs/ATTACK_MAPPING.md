# MITRE ATT&CK Mapping

IdentityGuard adds a limited reference to [Valid Accounts (T1078)](https://attack.mitre.org/techniques/T1078/) where privileged, dormant, or service-account access could be relevant to account abuse analysis.

The mapping gives analysts investigation context; a finding is not evidence that the technique occurred. IdentityGuard does not claim complete ATT&CK coverage. Mappings should be reviewed whenever rule semantics change.
